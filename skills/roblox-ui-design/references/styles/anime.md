# Anime RPG / gacha / tower defence / anime fighter

Fast, sharp, dramatic. Rarity drives the look. `depth: soft` leaning dark, `motion: snappy`.

- **Shape**: angular. Small radius (4-10) or cut corners and skewed parallelograms (draw them: `draw_assets.py shape chamfer2|skew`). Thin 1-2px light or gold trim instead of chunky dark outlines. Diagonal slashes as dividers.
- **Colour**: deep navy, charcoal or ink panels at 85-95% opacity; white text; one hot accent (crimson, electric blue, gold). Rarity gradients on unit frames, names and glows: grey, green, blue, purple, gold, red/prismatic.
- **Type**: a bold condensed or comic display face, often italic in feel (Bangers, Oswald:Bold, Sarpanch:Bold), with a thin dark stroke; a clean sans for stats. Big numbers.
- **Unit / item cards**: portrait art breaking out of the frame, a rarity band or corner tab, stars or a level chip, name on a dark gradient at the bottom.
- **HUD**: level and XP bar top-left; skill hotbar bottom-centre or bottom-right as squares with key labels and cooldown overlays; quest tracker right; menu icon row left or top. Tower defence: unit bar bottom-centre with costs, wave and money top.
- **Summon / banner screens**: featured unit large, single and x10 buttons side by side, pity counter small.
- **Signature details**: speed lines or halftone dots at low opacity, a slash-shaped highlight behind titles, rarity-coloured radial glow behind featured units.

**Make it this game's**: take the accent and motif from the source flavour (ninja = ink brush and vermilion, pirates = rope and brass, demon slayers = woodblock patterns). Keep the dark panel calm so rarity colours can shout.

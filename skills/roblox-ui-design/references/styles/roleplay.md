# Roleplay / life-sim / social hangout / dress-up

Friendly, tidy, out of the way; the player's avatar and house are the content. `depth: soft`, `motion: snappy` with a small overshoot.

- **Shape**: generous radius (16-24), circles and rounded squares for HUD buttons, real pills for chips and toggles. No dark outlines.
- **Depth**: soft tinted shadows under cards, or a 1-2px lighter/darker edge. Flat fills with a 3-6% gradient.
- **Colour**: white or pastel surfaces tinted toward the brand; one cheerful primary; dark warm-grey text on light, never black. Category colours pastel and few.
- **Type**: a rounded or geometric sans (Nunito, Montserrat, BuilderSans) at two weights; no text strokes on panels.
- **Icons**: simple two-tone or single-colour glyphs, consistent weight.
- **HUD**: a small cluster of round buttons on the bottom or right edge (backpack, phone, map, avatar); a money chip; everything else lives in a phone/tablet-style menu or a bottom sheet.
- **Menus**: grids of item cards with a picture, a name and a price chip; category tabs as pills or an icon rail; a search field.
- **Dress-up / fashion**: high-contrast serif or script display type, blush/black/white or iridescent pastels, thin gold lines, sparkles used sparingly, score cards.

**Make it this game's**: take the palette from the town or brand (seaside = sand, coral, sea-glass; city nightlife = plum and neon peach) and choose a container metaphor that fits (phone, catalogue, wardrobe tags).

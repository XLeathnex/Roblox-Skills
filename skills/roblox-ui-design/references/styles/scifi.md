# Sci-fi / cyberpunk / racing / sports

Precision instruments and broadcast graphics. `depth: flat`, `motion: snappy`.

- **Sci-fi**: dark blue-black or graphite panels at 80-95% opacity; 1-2px strokes in one signal colour; cut corners and corner brackets (`draw_assets.py shape chamfer|bracket`); fine grid, hex or scanline overlays at 90-96% transparency. Wide or mono type (Michroma, Jura, Sarpanch, RobotoMono) with small tracked caps labels and large numeric readouts. Glow only on the one live element. Choose the signal hue from the faction; cyan-on-black with glow everywhere is its own cliché.
- **Cyberpunk**: add a second clashing accent (magenta + acid yellow), glitchy offset duplicates of titles, warning-stripe bars.
- **Racing**: everything skewed 10-15 degrees the same way; heavy italic-feeling condensed numerals; speedometer and gear bottom-right, position ("3/8") top-left, lap and time top-centre; checker or stripe texture accents.
- **Sports**: a broadcast scorebug top-centre with team-colour blocks and a clock; bold condensed type; stamina and ability buttons bottom corners; replays and results as lower-third bars.
- **Shape**: 0-4 radius. Segmented bars (many small blocks) instead of smooth fills read as instruments.
- **HUD**: readouts at the corners, a thin frame of brackets, the centre empty.

**Make it this game's**: define the "manufacturer" of this interface (a mining corp, a racing league, a starship navy) and give it a logo mark, a number style and one signal colour used consistently.

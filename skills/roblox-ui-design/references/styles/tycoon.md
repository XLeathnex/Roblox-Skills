# Tycoon / management / business

Money is the hero. Two dialects; choose by the game's tone.

- **Casual dropper tycoon**: the simulator dialect turned down. `depth: chunky`, smaller lips (4-5), fewer colours, `motion: bouncy`.
- **Management tycoon** (restaurant, theme park, retail, city): a clean product UI. `depth: soft`, `motion: snappy`.
  - **Shape**: radius 8-12 on cards and buttons, 14-18 on panels. No outlines; soft tinted shadows or 1px hairlines.
  - **Colour**: white or deep neutral surfaces tinted toward the brand, one brand colour for primary actions, green only for money gained, red only for money you lack.
  - **Type**: a clean sans at two weights (Heavy for numbers and titles, Medium for labels). Tabular-looking numbers, right-aligned in lists.
  - **Icons**: flat single-colour glyphs tinted to the text colour.
- **Cash readout**: the largest thing on the HUD, with a "+$12/s" sublabel in a dimmer value. Affordable prices in the money colour, unaffordable in red.
- **HUD**: cash bottom-left or top-centre; build/shop toolbar bottom-centre with category tabs and a horizontal item strip; stats in a compact top row.
- **Lists**: rows with icon, name, a thin level bar, price button on the right. One primary button per row.

**Make it this game's**: take the brand colour and one material cue from what the tycoon sells (pizza = tomato red and kraft-paper tan, space mining = graphite and warning amber). Give the cash readout a custom plate that echoes it.

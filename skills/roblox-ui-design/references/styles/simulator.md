# Simulator / clicker / pet-collecting / brainrot

Loud, toy-like, instantly readable by a nine-year-old on a phone. `depth: chunky`, `motion: bouncy`.

- **Shape**: thick rounded objects. Radius 10-16 buttons, 14-20 cards, 20-28 panels. Square icon buttons in the side menu. Pills only for currency chips.
- **Edge**: one tinted dark outline everywhere (3 buttons, 4 panels) plus a hard bottom lip 5-10px. No soft blurry shadows.
- **Colour**: a calm panel body (cream, white, pale tint, or deep navy) carrying saturated buttons. Colour means function: one hue for buy/claim, one for close/danger, gold for premium/Robux. Rarity ladder is the only rainbow: grey, green, blue, purple, gold, then red/animated.
- **Type**: fat rounded display face in white with a dark outline and a hard drop shadow; numbers abbreviated (1.2M) and coloured like their currency. Body in a bold rounded sans.
- **Icons**: chunky outlined pictures (3D renders if the game has them), tilted 5-15 degrees and overflowing their frame. Never emoji.
- **Signature details**: title on a tilted ribbon overlapping the panel top; close button riding the top-right corner; sunburst or radial gradient behind items; diagonal stripes or dots at 88-93% transparency on headers; a rotated "NEW" or "x2" tag; stud texture for the brainrot/stud dialect.
- **HUD**: side menu left-middle, currency chips top or above the menu, big offers right-middle, auto/boost toggles bottom-centre.

**Make it this game's**: the cliché is Fredoka + blue-white panels + near-black outlines, and players read it as a cash-grab. Take the palette and outline hue from the game's theme (bakery = cream and chocolate, ocean = seafoam and deep teal, lava = charcoal and ember), pick the corner radius to match the world's blockiness, and choose one signature detail, not all of them.

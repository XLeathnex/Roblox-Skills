# FPS / competitive shooter / PvP

Information at a glance, nothing in the way of the crosshair. `depth: flat`, `motion: snappy`.

- **Shape**: rectangles with 0-6 radius, or a consistent skew/chamfer (draw them). No lips, no gloss. 1px strokes at most.
- **Colour**: translucent black plates (0.3-0.5 transparency), white text, and team or accent colours used as thin bars and fills: friendly cool, enemy warm. Dark same-hue outline on coloured text placed over the world.
- **Type**: heavy condensed numerals for ammo, health, score and timer (Oswald:Bold, Sarpanch, BuilderExtended); a clean sans for labels in small caps. Numbers are the biggest text on screen.
- **HUD**: score and timer top-centre; kill feed top-right; health and armour bottom-left (bar plus number); ammo and weapon bottom-right; minimap top-left under the Roblox buttons; ability icons near the jump button on touch. Keep a wide clear area around the centre.
- **Menus**: loadout and shop as flat cards with a coloured top or left edge for rarity; a left tab rail; one bright primary button (PLAY / DEPLOY).
- **Feedback**: 0.05-0.15s snaps; hit markers and damage numbers carry the juice.
- **Arcade shooters** (hero shooters, bedwars-likes) allow brighter fills and rounder corners but keep fills flat.

**Make it this game's**: pick the faction or setting (near-future PMC, neon arena, WW2, paintball) and take the accent pair, the corner treatment and the numeral face from it. A single consistent cut or skew angle across every plate is what makes it look designed.

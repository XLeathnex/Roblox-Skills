# Obby / tower / party / minigames

Almost no persistent UI; big, joyful moments between rounds. `depth: chunky` (party, kids) or `flat`/`soft` (towers, skill obbies), `motion: bouncy`.

- **HUD**: one stage or timer chip top-centre, coins top-right, a small menu button. Everything else behind one or two buttons. Keep the middle clear: players are jumping.
- **Shape**: bold simple chips and banners; radius to taste (blocky for classic obbies, round for party games).
- **Colour**: flat saturated fills on a dark translucent bar or a tinted dark outline; a different accent per minigame is fine if the frame stays constant.
- **Type**: a fat friendly display face for numbers and banners, outlined when over the world.
- **Moments**: round-start countdown that scale-pops each second; "STAGE 12" and "YOU WIN" banners as wide ribbons or skewed bars; a results list with placement medals; confetti.
- **Shop / skins**: a grid of cards with rarity edge colours, equip state as a check tag.
- **Tower / skill obbies**: quieter and cooler: thin text, neon accent, small timer; `depth: flat`.

**Make it this game's**: use the obby's signature colours and shapes (neon rainbow steps, lava and stone, candy) in the stage chip and banners. One strong banner design reused for every announcement beats many decorated panels.
